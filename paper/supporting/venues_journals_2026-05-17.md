## 5. Strategic Sequencing Recommendation

Given the EB-1A January 2027 filing window, the strongest sequencing is:

1. **Now → June 2026** — Pick ONE of: JSS "Software Quality Assurance for AI" (Aug 31) **or** IEEE Software "Edge-Cloud Continuum" (July 7). The Edge-Cloud SI is the strongest semantic fit because of the explicit "Observability and SRE/AIOps" topic; the JSS QA SI is the strongest empirical-fit because of the fault-injection benchmark. If bandwidth allows, submit to BOTH (different framings: Edge-Cloud emphasizes deployment + telemetry pipeline; JSS QA emphasizes benchmark + analysis modules + fault catalog).
2. **June → August 2026** — Submit the methodology + benchmark paper to **EMSE regular track** (rolling, no deadline pressure). Frame the 3,780-row benchmark + real-LLM validation as a stand-alone empirical contribution. Sister-submit a data paper to *Data in Brief*.
3. **September 2026** — Submit to **JSS "AI Techniques for Performance, Reliability, and Sustainability"** (Sept 30) with the cost-aggregation and reliability-analysis modules as the focus.
4. **October–December 2026** — Confirm TOSEM Agentic AI Special Collection status with EiC. If rolling, submit the comprehensive paper. If closed, submit to TOSEM regular track instead (same EB-1A evidentiary value).
5. **Parallel** — Submit to **TMLR** as a stand-alone experimental artifact paper for the real-LLM validation. TMLR's "technical correctness" criterion + flexible format is well-suited to an artifact-centric submission and provides an independent publication line.

By January 2027, this sequencing yields 3–5 distinct journal/SI submissions with at minimum 1–2 at "decision returned" stage — significantly stronger EB-1A original-contribution evidence than the conference-only portfolio.
